## Navigation & Spatial HUD

- **First-Visit Introductory Message:** Concise, dismissible first-visit onboarding overlay introducing Starmap, its spatial scales (`Galaxy` / `System` / `Planet`), primary controls, and quick entry points; dismissal persisted in `useSettingsStore`.
- **Local Night-Sky View to 3D Third-Person Projection (`FS-004`):** Enter an Earth location (lat/long or place) and optional time (defaults to "now") to view the local 2D night-sky dome rotated to that orientation, highlight/select visible stars in the sky view, and pull the camera out to a third-person 3D galactic projection to inspect the true 3D spatial distribution and depth of those stars.
- **Celestial Command Palette (`Cmd+K` / `/`):** Fast search-and-jump modal palette to locate stars, exoplanets, constellations, or toggle visual rendering layers. Not started — no prototype exists (the `Cmd+K` binding itself is part of the [`interaction-controls`](./tracks/interaction-controls_20261010/spec.md) shortcut registry). Natural home alongside the search surfaces ("Search scope & filters" below).
- **Zoom Ceiling & Boundary Clamp HUD Indicator:** Subtle, non-intrusive spatial HUD feedback when the camera reaches the active zoom ceiling (dense-catalogue horizon limit or device-tier cap) or any future spatial pan clamp, communicating why further zoom-out is bounded (e.g. dense survey horizon vs hardware ceiling) without blocking navigation.

## Data Architecture, Adaptive Search & Spatial Ingestion

- **Full-Catalogue System Expansion (Stage 1B & Stage 2):** Expand beyond the 100 pc GCNS + distant notable catalogues delivered by [`horizon-ceiling-and-notable-catalogue_20261121`](./tracks/horizon-ceiling-and-notable-catalogue_20261121/index.md) toward full-sky Gaia DR3/DR4 (~1.5B–2B stars). Re-validate architecture before scheduling; provisional two-stage direction:
  - **Stage 1B (~200 pc Gaia DR3 / ~2M–2.6M stars):** Pinned quality-cut 200 pc Gaia DR3 + Bailer-Jones snapshot; keep hot-path `partitions/*.bin` at 16 bytes/star and add a lazy aperture-only binary metadata sidecar (`meta/*.bin`, ~8 B/star, zero `JSON.parse`) to synthesise fallback summaries for anonymous stars; stream `.bin`/`meta/*.bin` in Node without holding millions of JS objects in RAM; move `partitions/` + `meta/` to external object storage/CDN (e.g. R2) with a compact spatial occupancy index outside `manifest.json`.
  - **Stage 2 (Full Milky Way / ~1.5B–2B stars):** Replace uniform 25 pc cubes with a 3D hierarchical octree LOD (~16k–32k stars/tile sorted by magnitude, solving Galactic Bulge single-sector overflow and pairing with *Zoomed-Out Macro Density View*); replace `fnv1a32` with exact 64-bit `BigUint64` IDs (`bigint` + namespace bit); replace Node builder with an out-of-core DuckDB/Rust pipeline joining Bailer-Jones Bayesian distances; implement scene-wide 64-bit floating origin; keep static search for named/notable systems with an edge-cached lookup API for raw Gaia IDs.
- **Adaptive Multi-Tier Search Strategy: Escalation & Device/Network Tiering (`FS-006`):** The core-first corpus prewarm is already shipped (`searchCorpusPrewarm.ts`, `asyncSearchExecutor.ts`, `searchRepository.ts`). Remaining: zero-result escalation (`core.json` first, auto-fetching `full.json` / category shards on zero matches) and device/network performance tiering for search (`navigator.deviceMemory`, `hardwareConcurrency`, `effectiveType`, `saveData`) — `deviceTierService` currently serves sector streaming only. *(Dynamic sector streaming graduated & archived: [conductor/archive/tracks/spatial-data-runtime_20261025/index.md](archive/tracks/spatial-data-runtime_20261025/index.md).)*
- **Direction-Only & Distance-Uncertain Deep-Sky Projections (`FS-018`):** Support galactic structures and deep-sky landmarks (`Green's SNRs`, `Barnard Dark Nebulae`, `OpenNGC` objects) that have 2D sky coordinates (`RA`/`Dec`) or broad distance uncertainty ranges rather than a single precise 3D parallax distance: represent them in the data model with direction vectors (`raDeg`, `decDeg`) and optional `[minDistPc, maxDistPc]` bounds, surface them in search and details telemetry with explicit uncertainty/sky-projection indicators, and render them in the 3D scene via radial line-of-sight rays, uncertainty frustums, or celestial sphere projections (bridging with `FS-004`).
- **Data Provenance Guide, Source Attribution & Contextual Panel/Datum Deep-Links (`FS-019`):** Author a comprehensive user-facing Data Provenance & Methodology Guide (`/guide/data-sources`) that names every astronomical catalogue and seed dataset (`Gaia DR3 GCNS`, `HIPPARCOS`/`HYG`, `RECONS 10 pc`, `NASA Exoplanet Archive`, `IAU Star Names & NameExoWorlds`, `AAVSO`, `Cantat-Gaudin`, `Harris Globulars`, `ATNF Pulsar Catalogue`, `OpenNGC`, `JPL Solar System Ephemerides & Small-Body Database`, `Curated Circumstellar Disks & Galactic Structures`), explains what parameters each source provides, documents how cross-matching, consolidation, and theoretical/inferred derivations (e.g. Keplerian $a$ from $P$, confidence levels) are combined in the pipeline, and links every telemetry panel and individual `<Datum />` / `<ConfidencePip />` to the exact section of that document explaining what the user is seeing at any given point. **Includes the pinned attribution footer** of the `DetailsPanel` full detent (astrometric baseline plus attribution deep-links to NASA Exoplanet Archive, SIMBAD, Gaia DR3 and Wikipedia — `DetailsAstrometrySection.tsx` currently shows only system ID, sector, coordinates and tags). *(Merged from archived `telemetry-container`.)*
- **Per-Details-Pane Catalogue Source Links & Layer Wikipedia Citations:** Surface direct external source links in every entity details pane (stars, planets, moons, minor bodies, structures) pointing to the exact upstream astronomical catalogue records supplying that entity's data (Gaia DR3, SIMBAD, NASA Exoplanet Archive, JPL SBDB, OpenNGC, ATNF, etc.), and add curated Wikipedia article source links to every dataset/canon/overlay layer. *(Pairs with `FS-019`.)*
