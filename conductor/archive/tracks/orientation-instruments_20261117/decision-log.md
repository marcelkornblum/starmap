# Decision Log: Orientation Instruments (`orientation-instruments_20261117`)

| ID | Date | Decision | Alternatives Considered | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `DEC-OI-001` | 2026-11-24 | **Upgrade `<Minimap />` and `<PreviewPanel />` stubs** rather than adding a separate third HUD compass box or duplicating `TargetReferencePlanePreview`. | Separate standalone HUD attitude widget leaving `<Minimap />` as a text table. | Both `<Minimap />` (`minimap-viewport-slot`) and `<PreviewPanel />` (`preview-canvas-slot`) were Stage 1 scaffolding stubs waiting for 3D orientation graphics; `camera-choreography` (#128) already shipped main-scene cross-scale datum transitions. |
| `DEC-OI-002` | 2026-11-24 | **Leave *Zoom Ceiling & Boundary Clamp HUD Indicator* in `conductor/backlog.md`.** | Fold zoom-clamp indicator into `<Minimap />`'s zoom bar. | Explicit user direction — a separate design is intended for that backlog item. |
| `DEC-OI-003` | 2026-11-24 | **3D-projected SVG rendering** for both `<Minimap />` and `<PreviewPanel />`. | Secondary WebGL `<Canvas>` or scissor-clipped `<View>` portals. | Zero extra WebGL contexts, zero scroll-clipping artefacts inside scrollable `<DetailsPanel />`, instant tokenised CSS Modules styling, and trivially unit-testable pure maths. |
| `DEC-OI-004` | 2026-11-24 | **`<Minimap />` uses `Map Moves` mode** (no `View Moves` frustum or mode toggle). | Fixed Galactic Plane with moving camera frustum (`View Moves`), or runtime mode toggle. | Validated live in PoC: rotating the curved Galactic Frame arc directly with the camera (`Map Moves`) gives immediate screen-parallel alignment with `CORE` and `ORB` without visual clutter. |
| `DEC-OI-005` | 2026-11-24 | **`<Minimap />` is framed on current position showing a zoomed-in curved annular slice of the Galactic Frame** (origin at Galactic Core) plus a **perpendicular north (`+Z` / `N`) stalk**. | Whole-galaxy 26 kpc disc centred on `CORE`; local Sol-centred concentric footprint. | Whole-galaxy view was too zoomed out; local concentric rings duplicated the main-scene `CartographicInstrument`. A zoomed-in polar sector centred on Galactic Core makes core curvature, `CORE`/`ORB` bearings, zoom footprint, and above/below-plane attitude (`+Z` stalk) unmistakable. |
| `DEC-OI-006` | 2026-11-24 | **`<PreviewPanel />` uses a fixed outer isometric viewpoint** in `<DetailsPanel />` `Partial` detent directly below `<DetailsActionControls />`, clicking navigates like `Details`. | Live camera-synced rotation; keeping preview in `Full` detent. | Validated live in PoC: a static outer viewpoint makes the relative tilt between outer and inner planes immediately comparable without spinning away; `Partial` detent makes it visible on first focus; clicking provides a natural drill-down affordance. |
| `DEC-OI-007` | 2026-11-24 | **`<PreviewPanel />` shows 2 light concentric circles + `CelestialSymbol` reticles** (zero real orbit lines, zero 50%-bright front/back split, zero tilt fin). | Real Keplerian orbit rings with 50% bright front arcs; plain dots; shaded `Δ` tilt wedge fin. | Validated live in PoC: 50%-bright real orbits and the tilt wedge fin cluttered the read; plain dots were hard to identify; authentic Starmap reticles on two light concentric circles make both plane tilt and body taxonomy immediately legible. |
| `DEC-OI-008` | 2026-11-24 | **Minimal borderless chrome** on both `<Minimap />` and `<PreviewPanel />`. | Nested card borders, inner SVG borders, duplicate title/scope headers, `HDG`/`ELV` readouts, caption strips. | Outer `SpatialShell` HUD container and `<DetailsPanel />` already frame both instruments; extra nested borders and redundant metadata created visual cruft. |
| `DEC-OI-009` | 2026-11-24 | **Real precalculated orientation where known, neutral (`0°`) where unmeasured.** | Keep Sol's `60.19°` fallback for all exoplanet systems; fabricate tilts from sky inclination `i` + `(l, b)`. | Exoplanet 3D orientation relative to the Galactic Plane is astronomically unconstrained without sky ascending node `Ω`. Precalculating Sol's `60.19°` and Solar System planetary `axialTiltDeg` in the build pipeline while leaving unmeasured systems neutral (`0°`) keeps the map scientifically honest and free of hardcoded `src/` literals. |
