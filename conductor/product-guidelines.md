# Product Guidelines

## Voice and Tone
- **In-Universe Utility:** The application is framed as a genuine utility for a fictional user with access to interplanetary travel. It should feel immersive and slightly sci-fi, but never "hammy" or overly dramatic. 
- **Accessible & Clear:** The amount of personality is minimal. Use simple, accessible language to explain complex astronomical concepts. 

## UX and Design Philosophy
- **Schematic Clarity:** The 3D models and maps should be schematic-based. Draw inspiration from iconic cartography (like the London Tube map or OS maps)—prioritize conceptual clarity and navigation over overwhelming the user with realistic scale or raw data dumps.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Hide complex data behind interactions. The map is the primary focus; rich data should be available on demand but never flood the screen by default.
- **Crisp & Characterful UI:** UI elements must be modern, clean, and crisp. They should have a clear design vision and character without being overly embellished (avoid the "wind tunnel" aesthetic).

## Interaction and Animation
- **Snappy Utility:** The app must feel responsive and functional first and foremost. 
- **Unobtrusive Animation:** Use short, subtle animations that enhance the experience without getting in the way (e.g., animate elements in, but not out). 
- **Systematic Consistency:** A consistent design system is critical. Easing curves, spacing, and transitions must be rigorously and consistently applied across all views.
